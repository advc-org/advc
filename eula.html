EULA

The advc End User License Agreement, version 1.0 — November 5, 2026.

1. The agreement

This agreement (“the EULA”) is between you and the advc project (“we”). It governs your use of advc — the compiler, the runtime, and the tools (“the engine”) — and the royalty that released games built with it pay.

The engine is free to use. This EULA is short on purpose, and it is numbered so that every clause has a place where it can be read as a whole. If something in this document is unclear, tell us and we will write it better.

2. Definitions

  • Product — one game built with the engine: the base game and all of its DLC together. Sequels and versions are separate Products (“Adventure I” and “Adventure II”); ports and remasters of the same game are the same Product.
  • Gross revenue — all amounts you actually receive from players (product sales, DLC, in-app purchases, subscriptions) plus payments you receive from advertising, less refunds and chargebacks.
  • advland sales — money you receive from sales of a Product that happen inside advland's own store.
  • Embedded product — a Product shipped inside another product, kiosk, platform, or bundle sold outside the usual storefronts.

3. The license grant

We grant you a worldwide, perpetual, non-exclusive license to use the engine to make games, to sell those games, and to keep every dollar your games earn — subject to the royalty in section 5.

You own your games: your source files, your assets, your save data, your players. Nothing in this EULA gives us rights over your content, over your names, or over your relationship with your players.

4. Embedded products

Embedded products are not covered by self-serve pricing or self-serve terms. Contact us and we will write terms with you.

5. The royalty

A royalty of 1% applies to a Product's lifetime Gross revenue above USD 10,000. advland sales are excluded: they neither owe the royalty nor count toward the threshold.

In plain words:

  • Under USD 10,000 lifetime Gross revenue: $0.
  • advland sales: $0 at any amount — the advland store fee already covers those, at any amount, forever. If a Product is sold on advland and elsewhere, the advland revenue is excluded from Gross revenue entirely and the rest counts normally.
  • The threshold is lifetime, per Product — it never resets annually.
  • Example: a Product earns USD 20,000 lifetime Gross revenue. The royalty is 1% × (20,000 − 10,000) = USD 100.

Gross revenue is not reduced by platform and store fees, payment-processing costs, ad-network fees, or production costs.

6. Reporting and payment — we trust our users

There are no statements, audits, or paperwork. When a Product crosses the threshold, you self-report and pay the royalty from the dev portal. We trust our users; the whole reason the rate is 1% and not 5% is that this clause is the entire enforcement mechanism.

7. The dev portal and feature votes

Royalties you pay convert into feature votes in the dev portal: developers who fund the engine steer which features get built. Votes never expire. The dev portal is coming, and its rules will be published when it arrives — but votes never expire, including votes earned before the rules are published.

8. Third-party platforms

When you distribute a Product through a storefront or platform, you follow their rules too. Their fees are theirs; our royalty is calculated on Gross revenue regardless of what any store deducts. If a platform's terms make the royalty impossible for you, talk to us before you give up.

9. Changes to this EULA

If this EULA changes, published games keep the version in force when they were first released for money. New terms apply only to games first released after the change takes effect. We announce any change at least 30 days before it takes effect, on the devlog. A royalty change never applies retroactively.

10. Termination

You may stop using the engine at any time, and this license ends then. The royalty terms (sections 5–6) survive for Gross revenue you received while your release carried this EULA — money earned under these terms is paid under these terms, and nothing you do later reopens it.

11. No warranties

The engine is provided “as is”, without warranty of any kind, express or implied — including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and non-infringement. You choose the engine; the games and their behavior are yours.

12. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, and our total aggregate liability to you under this EULA is capped at USD 100, regardless of how many Products or royalties are involved.
